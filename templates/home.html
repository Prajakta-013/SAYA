When you can't reach for help, SAYA reaches for you.

SAYA is an AI-assisted emergency-response network. The victim's smartphone can monitor for a suspected accident and automatically send the emergency to SAYA. SAYA prioritizes the incident, selects a SAYA ambulance, chooses a participating hospital, calculates the road route and ETA, and creates the assignment.

Open the phone monitor on the device you want to use for this prototype. Strong motion or rotation starts a 10-second cancellation countdown.
